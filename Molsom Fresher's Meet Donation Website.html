About The Event

Why Your Support Matters

The Molsom Fresher's Meet brings together students, leaders, elders, and alumni to honor our new batch entering higher education in Agartala. Your contributions directly cover logistics, awards, and student support.

Community Feast & Refreshments

Traditional cuisine lunch & tea session for over 300+ students and guests.

Allocated: ~40%

Cultural & Talent Shows

Stage setup, sound equipment, traditional costume rentals, and dance performances.

Allocated: ~25%

Fresher Kits & Awards

Welcome stationery packages, mementos for toppers, Mr. & Miss Fresher trophies.

Allocated: ~20%

Venue & Decorations

Auditorium booking fee, lighting, banners, welcome arches, and photography setup.

Allocated: ~15%
